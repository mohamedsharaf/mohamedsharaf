This week Universal Health Services, one of the largest hospital operators in the US, had to shut down computer systems across its facilities after a ransomware attack. Staff are using paper charts. Earlier this month, an attack on a university hospital in Germany forced it to turn away emergency patients.

In healthcare, an IT outage is a patient safety issue.

What any clinic or hospital should have:

  • Downtime procedures that people have practised: paper forms, printed schedules, how to order medicine.
  • Offline access to critical information like allergies and current medications.
  • Separate networks for medical devices.
  • Tested, offline backups of clinical systems.
  • MFA on remote access, and fast patching of VPNs and email.

Security teams and clinical teams need to plan this together.
