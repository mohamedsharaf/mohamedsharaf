Almost every week this year, researchers have reported another database left open on the internet: Elasticsearch, MongoDB, storage buckets, with millions of customer records and no password at all. Often it was a copy made for testing or analytics, forgotten.

No hacker needed. Search engines like Shodan index these within hours.

How to make sure it isn’t you:

  • Databases never listen on public IPs. Bind them to private networks and use firewall rules.
  • Authentication on, always, even for “temporary” or test instances.
  • Scan your own public IPs and cloud accounts for open database ports and public storage.
  • No production data in test copies, or mask it first.
  • Delete copies when the analysis or migration is finished.

The fix is boring configuration. The breach notice is not.
