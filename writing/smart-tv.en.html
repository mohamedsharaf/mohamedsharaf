Last week, before Black Friday, the FBI’s Portland office published advice on smart TVs. Many have microphones and cameras, and they connect to the internet. A TV with weak security can be a way into your home network, and in the worst case someone could watch or listen.

The advice, shortened:

  • Know what your TV has: search the model number for “camera” and “microphone”.
  • Change default passwords and turn off features you don’t use.
  • Cover the camera if you can’t turn it off. The FBI suggests black tape.
  • Install updates, and check whether the maker still releases them.
  • Read the privacy policy to see what data is collected about what you watch.

I’d add one: put smart TVs and other devices on a separate guest network, away from your laptops.
