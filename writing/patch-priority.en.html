Monthly update lists are now hundreds of items long. Small IT teams can’t test and install all of it in a day, and they shouldn’t try. They should patch in the right order.

  1. Anything on the internet edge: VPN, firewall, email gateway, remote access tools, file transfer servers, MDM. Within days, sooner if exploited.
  2. Anything known to be exploited: check CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list. If it’s there and you run it, it jumps the queue.
  3. Browsers and email clients: they open untrusted content all day. Let them auto-update.
  4. Servers inside the network, starting with domain controllers and backup systems.
  5. Everything else on a regular monthly cycle.

Keep a simple list of what you own in each group. When the next big bug drops, you already know where to look.
