Capcom, the maker of Resident Evil and Street Fighter, was hit by a ransomware attack earlier this month. The group behind it says it stole a large amount of data before encrypting systems, and threatens to publish it.

This is now the normal pattern: steal, then encrypt, then threaten to leak. Good backups help you restore systems. They don’t stop the data from being published.

So defence needs both:

  • Stop them getting in: MFA on remote access and email, fast patching of VPNs, blocking macros.
  • Notice them moving: alerts on new admin accounts, unusual logins and large data transfers out.
  • Limit what one account can reach, so the attacker can’t copy everything.
  • Know where personal data lives, so you can tell people quickly if it leaks.
