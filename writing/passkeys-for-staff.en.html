Today is World Password Day, so here’s my one suggestion: move your own staff to passkeys.

The breaches of the past few months mostly started the same way: a fake login page or a convincing phone call, a password typed in, an MFA push approved or an SMS code read out. Passkeys don’t work on a fake site, and there’s no code to read out.

Where to start:

  1. Admins and IT staff first, then finance, HR and anyone with access to customer data.
  2. Turn on passkey or security key sign-in in Microsoft Entra, Google Workspace or Okta. They all support it now.
  3. Register two per person: one on the laptop or phone, one hardware key as backup.
  4. Once a group is enrolled, remove SMS and push as fallback options for them.
  5. Fix the help desk process for lost devices, so it can’t be talked around.
