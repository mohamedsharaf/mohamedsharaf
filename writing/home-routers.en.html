Millions of people now work through the same home router that runs the kids’ games, the smart TV and a camera bought online. For most companies, that router is now part of their network, and nobody has looked at it.

Ten minutes, once:

  1. Change the admin password from the default on the sticker.
  2. Update the firmware, or turn on automatic updates if it has them.
  3. Use WPA2 or WPA3 Wi-Fi with a long password.
  4. Turn off remote administration and UPnP if you don’t need them.
  5. Put guests and smart devices on the guest network.
  6. If the router is very old and gets no updates, replace it. They’re cheap compared to an incident.

Companies can help: send a one-page guide, and offer to pay for a new router for staff who need one.
