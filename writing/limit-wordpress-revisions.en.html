WordPress saves a revision every time you update a post. On a busy site that becomes thousands of extra rows in the database. One line in wp-config.php controls it:

// Keep only the last 5 revisions of each post
define( 'WP_POST_REVISIONS', 5 );

// Or turn revisions off completely
define( 'WP_POST_REVISIONS', false );

Use straight quotes and a real false (no quotes around it). My original 2011 version had curly quotes copied from a blog, and PHP doesn’t accept those.

The setting only affects new revisions; old ones stay until you clean them up. Keeping a few is usually wiser than none: they have saved more than one person from a bad edit.
