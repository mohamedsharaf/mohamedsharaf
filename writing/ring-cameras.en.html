Over the past week, several families reported strangers talking to them, and to their children, through Ring cameras in their homes. Ring says its systems weren’t breached: the attackers logged in with email and password pairs leaked from other sites, on accounts without two-factor authentication.

That may be technically true. It’s still a design question. A camera inside a home shouldn’t be protected by a reused password alone.

If you have any home camera:

  1. Turn on two-factor authentication today.
  2. Use a unique password for the camera account.
  3. Check the shared users list and the login history in the app.
  4. Think about placement. Cameras facing bedrooms carry more risk than ones facing the door.

For device makers: make 2FA the default, and warn users when a login comes from somewhere new.
