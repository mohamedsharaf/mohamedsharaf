This month brought another round of extortion claims against companies’ cloud data platforms such as Snowflake and Databricks. Reports describe the same opening as earlier this year: voice phishing calls to employees, approved logins, then large queries and exports. Some of the numbers come from the attackers and aren’t confirmed. The method is consistent.

A data warehouse is where everything ends up: customers, orders, payments, analytics. It deserves your strongest controls.

  • Require SSO with phishing-resistant MFA for every human user. No local passwords.
  • Service accounts use key pairs or workload identity, with network policies limiting where they connect from.
  • Restrict access by network where the platform supports it.
  • Alert on unusual queries: huge result sets, exports to new stages, new users or roles.
  • Review who has access to raw personal data. Most analysts can work with masked columns.
