Google Authenticator finally backs up your codes to your Google account. Lose your phone, sign in on a new one, and your 2FA codes come back. Many people lost accounts over the years for lack of exactly this.

The catch: your codes now live with your Google account. Researchers also pointed out that the sync isn’t end-to-end encrypted yet, so Google could technically see them. Google says it plans to add that.

My take:

  • For most people, turn it on. Losing codes is a much more common problem than Google’s servers being breached.
  • Then protect the Google account like a vault: strong unique password, a passkey or security key, and recovery options you control.
  • For admin and finance accounts, keep codes off the cloud, or better, use hardware security keys.
  • Don’t keep the password and the code in the same place for your most important accounts.

Backups are good. Just know where your keys now live.
