The school year brings new learning platforms, a first phone for some, game accounts and group chats. Children reuse passwords, share them with friends and click on anything promising free game currency.

A short setup session, together with them:

  • Use the platform’s family features: Apple Family Sharing, Google Family Link, or Microsoft Family Safety. They handle age settings and purchase approval.
  • Use a password manager for the family, so each account has its own password, and you can reach them if needed.
  • Turn on two-step verification on their email. It resets everything else.
  • Set social and game profiles to private, and turn off location sharing in apps that don’t need it.
  • Explain the common scams: free skins or coins, “your account will be deleted”, strangers asking to move to another app.

The most useful rule is simple: if something online feels wrong or scary, tell us, and nobody gets in trouble for asking.
