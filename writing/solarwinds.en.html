The biggest security story of the holidays: attackers broke into SolarWinds’ build system and slipped a backdoor into updates for its Orion monitoring software, shipped between March and June 2020. Around 18,000 customers installed them. A much smaller number, including US government agencies and big tech companies, were then targeted further.

If you run Orion:

  • Follow the SolarWinds and CISA guidance, and update to the clean versions.
  • Check whether the affected versions ever ran, and whether the server talked to the domains listed in the advisories.
  • If it did, assume the network is compromised, starting with your identity systems.

For everyone else, the lesson is uncomfortable. Monitoring and management tools have admin rights everywhere. So:

  • Limit where they can reach, including the internet. A monitoring server rarely needs to browse the web.
  • Watch their behaviour, not just their files.
  • Know which vendors push code into your core systems.
