This month a security researcher showed that Zoom’s Mac app installed a small web server on the computer. It let websites start a Zoom call instantly, and a malicious site could join you to a call with your camera on. The server stayed even after you deleted Zoom.

Zoom removed it in an update, and Apple pushed a silent update that removes it from Macs.

The wider lesson is about convenience features:

  • Apps sometimes install helpers that keep running in the background, often to skip a click.
  • Check what’s listening on your machine now and then, and question anything you don’t recognise.
  • Turn off “join with video on” by default in meeting apps.
  • Uninstalling an app doesn’t always remove everything it added.
