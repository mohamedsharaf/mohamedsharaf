This year almost all holiday shopping is online, and scammers have built fake shops to match: copied designs, prices that look like a great deal, and ads on social media. You pay, and nothing arrives, or your card gets used elsewhere.

Before buying from a shop you don’t know:

  • Search the shop’s name plus “scam” or “reviews”.
  • Check the domain age and contact details. A site registered last month with no real address is a warning.
  • Prices far below everyone else are the bait.
  • Pay by card or a payment service with buyer protection, never by bank transfer or gift cards.
  • Type the address of big shops yourself instead of clicking ads.

And watch for “delivery problem” texts after you order. They’re the second half of the same season.
