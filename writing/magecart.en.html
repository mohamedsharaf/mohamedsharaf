The busiest shopping weeks of the year are also the busiest for card skimming. Groups known as Magecart inject JavaScript into checkout pages, often through a hacked plugin or a third-party script, and copy card details as customers type them.

The shop keeps working. Customers find out weeks later from their bank.

For anyone running an online shop:

  • Update the platform and plugins, and remove plugins you don’t use.
  • MFA on the shop admin and hosting accounts.
  • Keep the checkout page lean: fewer third-party scripts means fewer ways in.
  • Use a Content-Security-Policy that limits where scripts load from and where data can be sent.
  • Use the payment provider’s hosted fields or redirect, so card data never touches your page.
  • Monitor the checkout page for changes to its scripts.
