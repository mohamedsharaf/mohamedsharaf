Citrix Bleed (CVE-2023-4966) affects NetScaler ADC and Gateway. A request to the device makes it leak memory that contains valid session tokens. With a token, an attacker takes over a logged-in session, MFA already done.

The patch came out on 10 October. Since then, mass exploitation has started, and some attacks began weeks before the patch.

The catch: patching stops new leaks, but tokens already stolen keep working. So:

  1. Patch to the fixed version now.
  2. Then end every active and persistent session. Citrix lists the exact commands in its advisory. Everyone logs in again, and stolen tokens die.
  3. Look back at sessions from unusual IPs or with sudden IP changes since August.
  4. If you find signs of misuse, treat the internal systems those users could reach as possibly compromised.

Remote access gateways are a favourite target. Any time one leaks sessions, “patch and move on” leaves the door half open.
