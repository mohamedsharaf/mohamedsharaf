Citrix said last week that the FBI informed it of attackers inside its network. The likely method, according to the FBI: password spraying. That means trying a few common passwords, like Spring2019!, against many usernames, slowly enough to avoid lockouts.

It works because in any large company, someone has a weak password, and some login page doesn’t require MFA.

Defences:

  • MFA on every external login: email, VPN, remote desktop, web apps.
  • Block legacy protocols that skip MFA.
  • Ban common and breached passwords when people set them.
  • Alert on failed logins across many accounts from the same source, not just many failures on one account.

Lockout policies alone don’t stop spraying. It’s designed to stay under them.
