Millions of people now meet on Zoom every day, and uninvited guests have started joining public meetings and classes to shout and share offensive content. The FBI issued a warning about it yesterday.

Most of it comes from meeting links posted publicly, or meetings with no password.

Settings that stop most of it:

  • Require a meeting password, and share the link privately, not on social media.
  • Use the waiting room and let people in yourself.
  • Only the host can share the screen.
  • Lock the meeting once everyone has joined.
  • Use a new meeting ID for each meeting, not your personal one.
  • Keep the app updated. Zoom is fixing issues quickly right now.

For confidential meetings, ask what the tool encrypts and where the data goes, whichever tool you use.
