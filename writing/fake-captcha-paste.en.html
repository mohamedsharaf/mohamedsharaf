It looks like a normal check: “Verify you are human.” Then it asks you to press Windows+R, then Ctrl+V, then Enter. Or on a Mac, to open Terminal and paste something. The page has already copied a command to your clipboard. Running it downloads malware, usually an infostealer that grabs saved passwords and browser sessions.

This trick, often called ClickFix, works because people are used to jumping through hoops on websites, and nothing looks technical.

One rule for everyone: no real website will ever ask you to paste something into Run, PowerShell or Terminal. If one does, close the tab and tell IT.

For IT teams:

  • Show staff a screenshot of what it looks like. Seeing it once is the best protection.
  • Restrict the Run dialog and PowerShell for users who don’t need them.
  • Alert on PowerShell or mshta started from explorer.exe with a download command.
