Summer travel season means laptops left in taxis, phones stolen in cafés, and hotel Wi-Fi of unknown quality. A few habits make all of that much less dramatic.

Before you leave:

  • Make sure full-disk encryption is on: BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on Mac.
  • Install updates and back up, so a lost laptop is only an expense.
  • Remove files you won’t need on the trip, especially client data.
  • Know how to report a lost device to IT, and save that number somewhere other than the laptop.

On the road:

  • Use your phone’s hotspot or a VPN instead of open hotel Wi-Fi for work.
  • Lock the screen every time you stand up. Shut it down, don’t sleep it, before going through security.
  • Don’t plug into public USB charging points. Use your own charger.

Some borders can ask you to unlock devices. If you travel to sensitive places, take a clean loaner laptop.
