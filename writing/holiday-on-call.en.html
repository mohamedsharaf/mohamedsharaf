Ransomware groups have long preferred long weekends and holiday periods. Fewer people are watching alerts, decisions take longer, and the one person who knows the backup system is on a beach.

Before the summer break starts:

  • Write a simple rota: who is on call each week, and who backs them up.
  • Make sure the on-call person can actually reach the admin tools from where they’ll be, with MFA that works abroad.
  • Share one page with key numbers: IT provider, internet provider, insurer, incident response firm, management.
  • Check that alerts from backups, antivirus and the firewall go to someone who is working, not to a holiday autoreply.
  • Don’t schedule big changes or migrations for the week before people leave.

And tell your IT provider your holiday dates. Their coverage may be thinner than you think too.
