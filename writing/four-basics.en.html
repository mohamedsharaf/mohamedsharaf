It’s Cybersecurity Awareness Month, and you’ll see a lot of posters. This year CISA keeps it to four basics. They’re the right four.

  1. Turn on MFA for email first, then banking, social media and work accounts. An app or security key is better than SMS, and SMS is better than nothing.
  2. Use a password manager. Long, unique passwords, generated for you. You remember one.
  3. Turn on automatic updates for your phone, laptop, browser and apps. Most attacks use holes that already have a fix.
  4. Learn to spot and report phishing. Urgency, a request for a login or payment, a sender that’s slightly off. When in doubt, check through another channel.

If you manage a team, don’t send another quiz. Spend the month making these four easy: buy the password manager, switch on MFA for everyone, and give people one button to report a suspicious email.

Then thank the first person who uses it.
