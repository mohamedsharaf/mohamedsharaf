Earlier this month a group of hackers got into Verkada, a cloud security-camera company, and viewed live feeds from around 150,000 cameras: hospitals, schools, prisons, factories, Tesla and Cloudflare offices. Reports say they used super-admin credentials they found exposed on the internet.

One login with access to every customer. That’s the design flaw, more than the leaked password.

If you use cloud-managed cameras or building systems:

  • Ask the vendor who on their side can see your feeds, and how that access is logged and limited.
  • Turn on MFA and review users on your own account.
  • Think about where cameras point. Screens, keypads and whiteboards end up on video.
  • Put cameras on their own network, away from your office systems.

If you build a SaaS product: avoid god-mode accounts. Support access should be per customer, time-limited, approved and logged.
