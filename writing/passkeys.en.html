Turn on passkeys. Just don’t think the job is done.

A passkey is tied to the real website. A fake login page gets nothing from your phone, no matter how good it looks. There’s no password to reuse, leak or guess. That alone removes most account takeovers I see.

What it doesn’t fix:

  • Recovery. If “lost my passkey” falls back to an SMS code, your account is only as strong as that SMS.
  • Stolen sessions. Malware that copies your browser’s session cookie skips the login completely.
  • The old password. If the site still accepts it, it can still be phished.

What I do:

  1. Passkeys first on email, the password manager and cloud accounts. They reset everything else.
  2. Remove SMS recovery where I can. Recovery codes go somewhere offline.
  3. Register a second passkey or a hardware key as a backup.
  4. Admin and finance accounts get hardware keys, not synced passkeys.

After passkeys, the weakest door is usually recovery. Check it.
