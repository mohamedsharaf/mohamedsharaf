Police in several Texas cities are warning drivers about fake QR codes stuck on parking pay stations. Scan one and you land on a convincing payment page that takes your card details. The city never sees the money.

The pandemic made QR codes normal: menus, payments, check-ins. And a QR code is just a link you can’t read before you open it.

Habits worth having:

  • Look at the code itself. Is it a sticker on top of something printed? Walk away.
  • Read the address your phone shows before opening it. Does it match the city, restaurant or shop?
  • For payments, use the official app or website you find yourself, not the one the code sends you to.
  • Never enter passwords on a page you reached from a QR code in a public place.

For businesses printing QR codes: use your own domain, check your codes regularly, and tell customers what the real address looks like.
