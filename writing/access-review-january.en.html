Every audit I do finds the same thing: accounts that should have been closed months ago. A contractor from a 2024 project. A former employee’s mailbox that still forwards somewhere. An app connected to Google Workspace by someone who left.

January is quiet, and last year is fresh in everyone’s memory. Use it.

  1. Export the user list from your email platform, your VPN and your main SaaS tools.
  2. Send each manager the list of their people and ask one question: who shouldn’t be here?
  3. Check admin roles separately. There are usually more admins than anyone thinks.
  4. Review third-party apps with access to mail and files, and remove the ones nobody recognises.
  5. Disable first, delete a month later. It’s easier to undo.

It takes a day or two for a small company. It closes doors that attackers look for first.
