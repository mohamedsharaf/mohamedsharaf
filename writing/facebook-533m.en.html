Over the weekend, data on about 533 million Facebook users was posted for free on a hacking forum: phone numbers, names, locations, workplaces, sometimes emails. Facebook says it was scraped in 2019 through a feature it has since fixed.

Old or not, phone numbers rarely change. That makes this list useful for scammers for years.

What to expect and do:

  • More convincing SMS and WhatsApp scams that use your real name and employer.
  • SIM-swap attempts against people whose number protects important accounts. Add a PIN to your mobile account and move MFA off SMS where you can.
  • Check your exposure with breach notification services that now include this data.
  • Review what your profile shows publicly, especially phone number and who can look you up by it.

For companies: warn staff, especially finance and executives, that personalised messages are now cheap to make.
