Most small sites install a big analytics tool out of habit, then add a cookie banner because of it, then barely look at the reports. What they usually need is simple: how many visits, which pages, where people came from.

Privacy-friendly analytics give you that without following individuals around the web. Options include Cloudflare Web Analytics, Plausible, Fathom and self-hosted tools like Umami or Matomo configured without cookies. This site uses the first one.

What to look for:

  • No cookies or persistent identifiers, so in many cases no consent banner is needed. Check your local law.
  • Data not shared with advertising networks.
  • A small script that doesn’t slow the page down.
  • Data stored where your privacy rules require.

Fewer third-party scripts also means fewer things that can be hijacked to inject code into your pages.
